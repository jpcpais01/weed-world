# Patch notes

## 23 Oct 2026 · 22:00 UTC
- **Gather plants.** Press grab (E, X on gamepad, or the hand button) and your Keling bows down, stretches out with fingers spread, closes its hand around the nearest herb, plucks it and brings it up for a sniff. It reaches low tufts, ledges and hanging vines.
- **A fixed face.** The head no longer twists or squashes your Keling's face. It now turns only a little, the way a creature with no neck should.
- **Buttercups in the crown.** The dandelion puffs are now cupped yellow buttercups that bob in the leafy hair.
- **No more backpack.** The basket on the back is gone.
- **Smoother moves.** Arms bend at the shoulder without stretching the whole body, jogging and cheering look calmer, and hard landings end in a crouch instead of a roll.

## 23 Oct 2026 · 21:00 UTC
- **Meet your Keling!** Your little forest creature now idles, walks, jogs and sprints with smooth blends between every pace, so planted feet stay put.
- **Turn, skid and leap.** Turn on the spot, skid to a stop when you change direction at speed, and jump higher the longer you hold. Big drops end in a roll.
- **Alive in every frame.** Breathing, head turns, leaning into corners, feet that settle on slopes, and a crown of stalks and a basket that bounce as you move.
- **Play your way.** Touch joystick and buttons on phones (push the stick past its ring to sprint), plus keyboard, mouse and gamepad. Try the emotes: dance, cheer, sit and nod.
- **Test grounds.** An open meadow with jump blocks, ramps, a tower to drop from, a slalom and a bumpy slope to try every move.
