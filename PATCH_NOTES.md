# Patch notes

## 24 Oct 2026 · 15:30 UTC
- **Wild weed in the Whispering Glade.** Three strains grow in little patches beside the paths: warm Ember Kush, violet Starlit Haze and fresh Verdant Dream. The first patch grows just ahead of where you start. Walk up and press grab (E, X on gamepad, or the hand button) to pluck a glowing bud. The plant shivers, sheds a few leaves and slowly grows the bud back, and every new strain you find gets its own little fanfare.
- **Roll your own.** With a bud in your pouch, press R (right trigger, or the new leaf button). A little forest magic floats the bud and a rolling paper up between your Keling's hands: the bud crumbles onto the paper, the paper curls itself up, gets a lick and a twist, and ten seconds later, ta-da, a joint ready to smoke. Walk off halfway and you keep your bud.
- **Light up and puff.** Press again to snap a fairy flame off your fingertips and light it. Each press is one deep puff, three to a joint: the ember flares, your Keling's chest swells, and out rolls a cloud of smoke in the colour of the strain. Between puffs a thin curl of smoke rises from the joint in your hand.
- **Bench powers.** Smoke while sitting on a scenic bench and the view gives you a power: Brook Stride by the river (run faster with ripples at every step), Spirit Sight at the Lotus Pond (every ripe bud shines through the trees), Featherfall at Misty Falls (jump twice as high and drift down), Mountain Might on Sunset Ridge (grow big and land with a stomp) and Bloom Trail in Elder's Meadow (flowers spring up in your footsteps and nearby plants ripen). Anywhere else you get the Glade Glow: the forest turns dreamy and rainbow motes drift around you. Every puff makes it stronger and last longer.
- **Your stash.** A pouch under the logo shows your buds and your joint with the puffs left (tap it or press Q to choose which strain to roll). It's saved, so it's all still there next time you play.

## 24 Oct 2026 · 13:00 UTC
- **Trees that grow out of the ground.** Trunks now splay into mossy surface roots that dive into the soil, standing in a ring of bare earth and moss with a soft shadow where they meet the floor. Ferns, clover and taller grass gather round their feet instead of growing straight through the bark.
- **Rocks that sit in the earth.** Boulders, stepping stones and path stones fade into the exact colour of the ground beneath them, with soil and moss creeping up their sides and a damp contact shadow at the base. Stones are a warmer, lighter grey, so they no longer look like black holes in the meadow.
- **Paths you'd want to walk.** Footpaths have a crisp, wandering edge with a damp dark lip, a paler trodden middle and a few small stones that catch the sun. Grass grows right up to the dirt and is short, splayed and sun-bleached along the verge, with clover patches where feet have pressed it flat, instead of a tall green wall.
- **One floor, everywhere.** Logs, stumps, roots and grass blades all share the ground's own soil, moss and dirt colours, steep ground shows earth and short turf instead of flat green, no grass grows under the water, and far meadows keep the colour of their grass after the blades fade with distance.

## 24 Oct 2026 · 12:00 UTC
- **Weed World updates itself.** A few seconds into play, the game checks for a newer version. If there is one, a little sprouting badge slides down with the news, and tapping it shows everything new since your version. Tap Update now and a vine grows across the card as it downloads, with the size, speed and time left. Tap Keep playing and it carries on in the background while a small chip at the top tracks it.
- **Safe and simple on every device.** Each download is checked against the release's fingerprint before anything installs. On Android, the system installer opens next and your progress stays put. The very first time, the card shows you the one switch Android asks for, then picks up by itself when you come back. On Windows, the game swaps in the new version and reopens on its own. After updating you're welcomed to the new version, and "Check for updates" sits at the bottom of the What's new scroll.
- **Solid tree stumps.** The old cut stumps were missing their tops, so you could see straight through them. Now they're solid, with a sawn top showing warm growth rings, a dark heart, cracks and a mossy rim.

## 24 Oct 2026 · 03:00 UTC
- **Five scenic benches.** Little Keling-sized benches now wait at the best views in the Whispering Glade: a silvered driftwood log on the riverbank, a cream-and-teal garden bench by the Lotus Pond, a mossy stone seat facing Misty Falls, a grand four-seat sunset bench with a glowing lantern on the ridge, and a bench the forest grew from living roots and boughs in Elder's Meadow. Trodden trails lead out to them, and each one has its own little bench badge on the minimap and the big map.
- **Sit and take it in.** Walk up and press grab (E, X on gamepad, or the hand button) when the prompt floats up. Your Keling trots over, turns round and hops up onto the seat, little feet dangling and kicking happily. The camera drifts round to frame the view and the bench's name fades in. Move, jump or press grab again to hop back down.
- **Every view has a spirit.** Each bench carries a carved emblem for what it looks out on (water, earth, air, spirit or life) that glows and sparkles while someone sits there. Remember them: they'll matter soon.

## 24 Oct 2026 · 02:00 UTC
- **A map of the whole glade.** A round minimap now sits in the corner, painted from the forest itself: rolling hills with contour lines, worn footpaths, the pond, the creek and its waterfalls, and every tree crown in its own colour (pink blossoms, golden autumn leaves, dark pines and the lilac Elder). It turns with your camera, so up is always where you're looking, and a little N bead keeps north in sight. Scroll over it to zoom.
- **The big map.** Press M (Y on a gamepad), or tap or click the minimap, and the map swoops out from your Keling to show the whole valley with every place named. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap the target button to find yourself again.
- **Waypoints.** Tap any place or spot on the big map to set a waypoint. A golden beam of light rises from it in the forest, a dotted trail and the minimap point the way with the distance, and it clears itself when you arrive.
- **Find named places.** Wander into the Blossom Grove, Glowshroom Hollow, the Sunset Overlook or seven other spots and their name drifts onto the screen. Where you are always shows under the minimap, and friends will appear on both maps as coloured Kelings once co-op arrives.
- **A cozier HUD.** New rounded lettering, frosted glass panels, key-cap control hints that tuck away into a small chip (H brings them back), a leafy "What's new" scroll with a dot when there is news, and warmer touch buttons.
